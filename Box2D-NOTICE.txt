Joint constraints in Module/Joints2D.sx are adapted from Box2D 3.1.1
src/solver.h, src/{distance,prismatic,revolute,weld,wheel}_joint.c,
src/math_functions.c and include/box2d/math_functions.h, commit
8c661469c9507d3ad6fbd2fea3f1aa71669c2fe3. Step-relative displacement and
normalized rotation integration in Module/World2D.sx follow src/solver.c
and include/box2d/math_functions.h at the same revision. Speculative contact
margin, contact frequency caps and post-solve hit criteria follow src/constants.h,
src/world.c and src/solver.c at that revision.
Geometry in Module/Geometry2D.sx is adapted from src/geometry.c,
src/distance.c, src/hull.c and src/manifold.c at the same pinned revision.
The chain/circle manifold uses adjacent ghost vertices to reject endpoint
regions owned by neighboring chain segments.
This includes analytic ray intersections and initial-overlap shape casts.
Motor/mouse preparation and solving in Module/Joints2D.sx follow
src/motor_joint.c and src/mouse_joint.c at that pinned revision
(Copyright (c) 2023 Erin Catto). Inter-substep warm starting follows src/solver.c.
Box2D is not linked into the Silex runtime.
